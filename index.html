What does Spine do?

We find the right leader, then prove the hire works.

Octopus Legacy went from about 40 people to 200 in a few years, and the leadership team is now catching up with the business. The VP Sales / Revenue sets how the partnership channel and its team of about 20 scale towards 50,000 cases.

A hire at this level is too expensive to judge on a CV and a good final round. Spine puts every hire in writing: what the evidence says before the offer, how the person is tracking once they start, and what that teaches you about the next hire.

Evidence-led decision making

One decision report per final candidate and a side-by-side final round review. Every claim cites its evidence.

Calibrate & validate your hire

A calibration report at Day 30, 60 and 90 against the outcomes agreed at kick-off: on track, off track, and what to change.

Building your hiring pulse

Each verdict feeds Octopus Legacy’s hiring pulse, so the bar for the next hire is written from evidence.

Calibrate & validate your hire

We validate the hire alongside you for 3 months.

Most leadership hires that fail show it in the first three months, long after a recruiter has been paid and gone. For a VP Sales / Revenue we track three signals from Day 1: partners signed, referral cases flowing from those partners, and the AE and AM hires made and ramping. Each calibration report says whether the hire is on track and what to change.

Building your hiring pulse

Every hire makes your next one sharper.

Each hire builds the hiring pulse for who works well at Octopus Legacy: which archetypes succeed, which warning signs were real, and what the next seat needs. It takes on average 3-5 leadership hires to build a predictive & tailored hiring pulse.
